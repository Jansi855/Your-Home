Explore government schemes and benefits for home buyers and real estate investors.

Save more with the right scheme Check your eligibility and unlock benefits

PMAY (Urban)

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban)
Affordable Housing

Interest subsidy on home loan for eligible beneficiaries in urban areas.

Interest Subsidy Up to 6.5% on Home Loan
Benefit Upto ₹2.67 Lakh Direct Credit
Who can apply? Families with income up to ₹18 LPA

Home Loan Tax Benefits

Income Tax Act
Tax Benefits

Save tax on home loan principal and interest under Sec 80C & 24(b).

Deduction Upto ₹1.5 Lakh on Principal (Sec 80C)
Deduction Upto ₹2 Lakh on Interest (Sec 24b)
Who can apply? Individual home loan borrowers

PMAY (Rural)

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin)
Affordable Housing

Financial assistance for construction of pucca house in rural areas.

Assistance Upto ₹1.20 Lakh per house
Interest Subsidy 3% on Loan Amount
Who can apply? Rural families with eligible income

CLSS

Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme
Loan & Finance

Interest subsidy for EWS / LIG home buyers.

Interest Subsidy Up to 6.5% on Home Loan
Benefit Upto ₹2.67 Lakh Lump-sum Subsidy
Who can apply? EWS / LIG category home buyers

Section 80EEA

Affordable Housing Extra Deduction
Tax Benefits

Additional tax deduction of ₹1.5 Lakh on home loan interest for first-time buyers.

Extra Deduction ₹1.5 Lakh Over Sec 24(b)
Stamp Value Limit ₹45 Lakh Affordable Cap
Who can apply? First-time buyers of affordable housing

Women Duty Concession

State Government Stamp Rebates
Tax Benefits

1% to 2% discount on property stamp duty when registered in female name or jointly.

Duty Discount 1% – 2% on Registry Cost
Avg Savings ₹1 – 2.5 Lakh Instant Rebate
Who can apply? Female sole or co-owners

Housing Board Lottery

MHADA / DDA / KHB / CIDCO
Affordable Housing

Direct lottery allocation of subsidized, legally clear government residential flats.

Price Discount 20% – 40% Below Market Price
Allocation Draw of Lots 100% Transparent
Who can apply? Resident citizens (EWS/LIG/MIG)
